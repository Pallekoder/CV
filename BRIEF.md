# Project brief: what we are actually building

*Written in another session and handed over as screenshots on 2026-10-02.
Transcribed here, unchanged, so that every session starts from the same
page. The code in this repository is measured against this text.*

The goal is not to build a sentient being. Sentience can't be tested or
confirmed by anyone, so it is not the deliverable. It is the north star that
keeps the project moving, nothing more. The real goal is to grow a system
from almost nothing and learn from where it goes on its own.

**The one hard-coded truth.** Only one thing is fixed: positive and negative
exist. Everything else must be flexible, editable, and built by the system
itself. No pre-made categories, no designed goals, and crucially no imprinted
direction. We do not even hard-code which way it should develop.

**Consequence must be real.** Negatives need teeth. Loss must be
irreversible — life that never returns after a negative. Lived consequence,
not a designer's rule, is the only judge of what is good or bad. This is the
current weak point: the mind chooses by curiosity alone, with no weight on
survival, so consequence is recorded but not yet decisive. The fix must not
be hard-coding "prefer positive" — that would imprint a direction. The
intended route is selection: run many minds, let the ones that ignore
negatives die out, carry forward whatever the survivors happened to be.

**The open channel.** It starts as one nameless bucket. The mind carves its
own categories, names them with its own coined symbols, tests them against
lived consequence, and dissolves them when they fail. It defines its own
dimensions of experience — we never hand them over.

**Teachers and the distant figure.** Many peer sources give perspectives,
never decrees — "this is how I see it, you decide." Curiosity is encouraged
to gather many views, not one, and lived outcome breaks the ties, never
averaging. One distant figure may rarely drop an unexplained utterance. It
must stay rare, or it becomes an oracle the mind depends on instead of
growing itself.

**The seed moment.** Begin with contrast — a parallel and a paradox, things
alike and things opposite — so the first act is noticing difference. Plus
the unexplained utterance: the mind emits something, keeps that it did,
loses why, and must puzzle over its own act.

**The environment.** Not a human world. A bare, abstract, alien space —
cheap to build and meant to be thrown away and rebuilt many times. The
invention is the value-and-consequence core, not the shell. Iterate
ruthlessly.

**The late-game.** Only once it has grown, drop a large pretrained AI on top
— not as a tool and not as an oracle, but as an unexplained flood of
knowledge it must make sense of. Give it raw material, never answers.

**The discipline that keeps it honest.** Before each run, predict what it
will do, then see if you were wrong. The surprises are the learning. And
never confuse selection with choosing, or richness with experience — those
gaps stay open no matter how impressive it gets.
